TREE-LEVEL SEVERITY GRADING INSTRUCTIONS
=========================================

For each tree, open its contact sheet (contact_sheets/<tree>_contact_sheet.png).
It shows ALL photos taken of that one tree, numbered, with NO model
predictions or overlays -- please grade based on your own visual judgement only.

For each disease you can see present anywhere on the tree (rough bark
and/or stripe canker), assign ONE overall treatment stage for that
disease considering the tree as a whole -- not per photo:

  P = Preventive          (no/trace disease, monitor only)
  E = Early control       (limited, localised disease)
  A = Active management   (significant, spreading disease)
  S = Severe outbreak     (majority affected / girdling risk)

Fill in the 'expert_stage' column in tree_grading_sheet.csv with P/E/A/S.
If a disease is NOT present on a tree at all, leave that row blank.
Use 'expert_notes' for anything relevant (e.g. 'looks like it may be
girdling on one side', 'hard to tell from photos, would want field visit').

Please grade blind: do not look at any pipeline output before grading.
